Our Care Services

Personalized assisted living in a warm, home-like environment

Our 6-bed residential care home is dedicated to providing compassionate, high-quality care that promotes dignity, independence, comfort, and safety for every resident. We offer individualized care plans tailored to each resident's needs while providing the warmth and attention of a family-centered environment.

Our Commitment

“We are committed to creating a safe, comfortable, and nurturing environment where seniors can enjoy the highest quality of life while receiving the personalized care and attention they deserve.”

What guides us

1

Mission

To provide compassionate, personalized, and quality care in a safe, comfortable, and family-oriented environment where every resident is treated with dignity, respect, and kindness while enjoying the highest possible quality of life.

2

Vision

To be a trusted leader in residential elder care, creating a warm and nurturing home where seniors can age with dignity, maintain their independence, and experience meaningful connections with caregivers, family, and community.

3

Our Promise

Individualized care plans tailored to each resident's needs, delivered with the warmth and attention of a family-centered environment. We treat your loved ones like our own.

Services We Provide

Everything your loved one needs, under one roof

Around-the-clock care, home-cooked meals, and close coordination with the people who matter in each resident's health.

01

24-Hour Personalized Care & Supervision

  • Around-the-clock caregiver assistance
  • Continuous monitoring for resident safety and well-being
  • Assistance with daily routines and activities

02

Assistance with Activities of Daily Living (ADLs)

  • Bathing and personal hygiene
  • Grooming and dressing
  • Toileting and incontinence care
  • Mobility and transfer assistance
  • Eating and feeding assistance as needed

03

Medication Management

  • Medication reminders and assistance
  • Medication administration by trained staff
  • Coordination with physicians and pharmacies

04

Nutritious Home-Cooked Meals

  • Three healthy meals daily
  • Nutritious snacks provided throughout the day
  • Accommodation for special dietary needs and physician-ordered diets

05

Housekeeping & Laundry Services

  • Regular housekeeping and room cleaning
  • Linen changes
  • Personal laundry services

06

Health Monitoring & Care Coordination

  • Observation and reporting of health changes
  • Coordination with physicians, home health agencies, hospice providers, and other healthcare professionals
  • Assistance scheduling medical appointments
